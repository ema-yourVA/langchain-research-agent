# n8n vs Make vs Zapier freelance demand and pay in 2026

**In short:** The sources I read point to two things. Zapier has the most job listings from small businesses. n8n is the fastest-growing skill, and it gets the biggest projects. Make sits in the middle. Pay depends more on skill level and project size than on which tool you use. I found no hard, independent head-to-head count of job postings, so treat the ranking as indicative.

## Key findings

**Demand**
- One freelance platform had more than 1,400 open Zapier-related jobs in early 2026. The article doesn't name the platform. [1]
- Delivvo says Upwork lists 1,238 open n8n jobs. It calls n8n the biggest shift in freelance automation over the past 18 months. It does not give matching counts for Make or Zapier. [2]
- The two counts above come from different sources, dates and platforms. They can't be compared directly. They do suggest Zapier and n8n are both in heavy demand.
- A scrape of 1,000+ Upwork AI automation jobs, reported on Reddit and summarized by Tallyfy, listed these top skills: Python, n8n, Make, Zapier, web scraping and CRM work. Most buyers wanted simple "connect this to that" work, not autonomous AI agents. [3]
- Upwork's 2026 skills report, as quoted by Tallyfy, shows demand for AI-integration skills up 178% year over year. AI skills overall are up 109%. [3]
- Delivvo says Zapier suits non-technical small business clients. n8n suits technical or compliance-sensitive clients, because it can be self-hosted. Make suits clients who want a visual editor for complex flows. [2]
- Delivvo says Make is getting squeezed. Small businesses default to Zapier, and mid-market clients increasingly ask about n8n. This is one blog's view, not hard data. [2]

**Pay**
- The generalist lane on Upwork averages about $39/hr across all skills. Zapier setup work mostly bills at $30 to $75/hr. It is often fixed-price at $500 to $2,500 per workflow. [2]
- Specialists with a portfolio in n8n or Make charge $75 to $150/hr. Multi-workflow projects run $3,000 to $15,000. [2]
- Senior platform engineers charge $150 to $300+/hr, mostly on retainer. A typical example is self-hosted n8n behind a security audit. [2]
- Delivvo puts n8n projects at $5,000 to $25,000, with maintenance retainers of $1,500 to $4,000 a month. Zapier setups for one niche run $1,500 to $3,500 each. [2]
- UlexAI gives service tiers that don't depend on tool. Audits cost $1,500 to $3,000. Implementations cost $3,000 to $15,000. Managed automation costs $800 to $3,000 a month. [4]
- US full-time salaries for no-code automation specialists run from the mid-$60,000s to about $130,000. Integration engineers, who work with APIs, are among the best paid at about $120,000 to $125,000. Freelance rates on platforms range from small fixed fees to $100/hr or more. [1]

**Cautions**
- All the pricing figures come from blogs and affiliate-style guides, not primary data. UlexAI says outright that it earns affiliate commissions. [4]
- The Neura Market comparison has errors. It says Make was acquired by Celonis in 2024, and it says n8n has no native AI agent support. I wouldn't rely on it for tool facts, so I didn't use it for any findings. [5]
- The Delivvo page lost several of its figures when I read it. It also says Zapier has 8,000+ integrations, while Neura says 7,000+. [2][5]

## What to do with this
1. Learn Zapier and Make basics first if you want quick small-business work. Specialize in n8n, including self-hosting and code nodes, if you want larger projects and higher rates.
2. Check live demand yourself. Search Upwork for "n8n", "Make.com" and "Zapier" and compare the open job counts and the number of proposals per job.
3. Sell outcomes, not tools. Offer fixed-price packages, such as a CRM-to-email build or a lead-routing system. Add a monthly retainer for monitoring, because buyers worry about workflows that break silently. [3]
4. Add Python or API skills. Integration work pays more than pure no-code work. [1]
